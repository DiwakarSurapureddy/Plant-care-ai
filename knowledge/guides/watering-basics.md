# General Plant Watering Guide

## Overview

Proper watering is crucial for indoor and outdoor plant health. Most plants suffer more from persistent overwatering than temporary underwatering.

## Soil Moisture Testing

Always test the top 1 to 2 inches of soil with your finger or a moisture meter before watering. If the soil is moist and clings to your skin, wait before adding more water.

## Drainage and Pots

Always ensure growing containers have functional drainage holes. Never let pots sit in standing trays of stagnant runoff water.

## Signs of Overwatering vs Underwatering

- Overwatering: Yellowing leaves, soft and mushy stems, musty odor, fungal growth, root rot.
- Underwatering: Dry brittle leaf edges, wilted leaves that perk up within hours of watering, shrunk soil pulling away from the pot rim.
