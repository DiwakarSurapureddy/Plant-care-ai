# Nursery Pricing and Stock Record Template

## Overview

This note defines the structured format for adding real nursery price and availability data to the Plant Care AI knowledge base.

## Pricing Policy

Nursery prices fluctuate by region, nursery provider, pot size, and season. Never fabricate or assume prices without a verified receipt or official catalog entry.

## Verified Fields

- Plant Name: Common name and botanical name
- Pot Size: e.g., 4-inch nursery pot, 6-inch hanging basket, 10-inch ceramic planter
- Price: Numerical value in specified currency
- Stock Status: In stock, Seasonal, Out of stock
- Verification Date: Date confirmed with the nursery
