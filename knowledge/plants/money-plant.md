# Money Plant Care

## Plant Overview

Money Plant is a popular indoor plant. It is commonly grown in homes and offices.

## Watering

Water the plant when the top layer of soil feels dry. Avoid keeping the soil continuously waterlogged.

## Sunlight

Place the plant in bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid prolonged exposure to harsh direct sunlight.

## Soil

Use a well-draining potting mix and a pot with drainage holes.

## Growing Method

Money Plant can be propagated using healthy stem cuttings. Place a cutting with a suitable node in water or an appropriate growing medium and monitor root development.

## Common Problems

Yellow leaves can be caused by overwatering, poor drainage, or unsuitable light conditions. Check the growing conditions before deciding on a solution.
