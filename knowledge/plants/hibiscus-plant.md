# Hibiscus Plant Care

## Plant Overview

Hibiscus is a flowering plant commonly grown in home gardens and nurseries.

## Watering

Water when the upper layer of soil begins to dry. Avoid waterlogging.

## Sunlight

Hibiscus generally grows well in a sunny location with several hours of sunlight.

## Soil

Use fertile, well-draining soil.

## Growing Method

Hibiscus can be propagated using suitable stem cuttings or nursery saplings.

## Fertilizer

Use a suitable flowering-plant fertilizer according to the product instructions.

## Common Problems

Check for yellow leaves, pests, and poor flowering. Review watering, sunlight, and soil conditions before deciding on a solution.
