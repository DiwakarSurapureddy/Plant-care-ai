# Tulsi Plant Care

## Plant Overview

Tulsi is a popular aromatic plant commonly grown in Indian homes and gardens.

## Watering

Water when the top layer of soil starts to dry. Avoid excessive watering.

## Sunlight

Place Tulsi in a location with adequate sunlight.

## Soil

Use well-draining soil suitable for growing herbs.

## Growing Method

Tulsi can be grown from seeds or suitable stem cuttings, depending on the variety.

## Fertilizer

Use compost or a suitable plant fertilizer in appropriate amounts.

## Common Problems

Check for yellow leaves, pests, and poor growth. Review sunlight, watering, and drainage conditions.
