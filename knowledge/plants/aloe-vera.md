# Aloe Vera Plant Care

## Plant Overview

Aloe Vera is a succulent plant commonly grown in pots and home gardens.

## Watering

Allow the soil to dry before watering again. Avoid excessive watering because it can cause root rot.

## Sunlight

Provide bright light and introduce the plant to direct sunlight gradually.

## Soil

Use a well-draining succulent or cactus potting mix.

## Growing Method

Aloe Vera is commonly propagated using healthy offsets, also called pups, growing near the parent plant.

## Fertilizer

Fertilizer is usually needed sparingly. Follow the instructions for a suitable succulent fertilizer.

## Common Problems

Soft leaves or rotting roots may indicate excessive watering. Check soil drainage and the plant's growing conditions.
