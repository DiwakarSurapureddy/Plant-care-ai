# Rose Plant Care

## Plant Overview

Rose is a flowering plant grown in gardens and nurseries.

## Watering

Water the soil when needed and avoid waterlogging. Adjust watering based on weather and soil moisture.

## Sunlight

Roses generally grow well with several hours of direct sunlight each day.

## Soil

Use well-draining soil enriched with suitable organic matter.

## Growing Method

Roses can be grown from suitable stem cuttings or nursery saplings. Use healthy planting material and appropriate growing conditions.

## Fertilizer

Use a suitable fertilizer according to the plant's growth stage and product instructions.

## Common Problems

Roses may develop pests, fungal diseases, or yellow leaves. Check the plant carefully before selecting a treatment.
